About the center

Built as a memorial, run as a classroom

The center carries the name of the late Shri Harihar Prasad and operates as a Community Service Center under the Datamation Foundation Charitable Trust, offering short, practical courses to residents of Ekdanga and the surrounding gram panchayats.

Most learners arrive by word of mouth or a pamphlet left at a shop counter. They stay for six months, come out with a certificate and a trade, and — the center hopes — a way to earn that didn't exist for them before.

The center runs two tracks side by side: a computer lab for digital literacy, and a stitching room for tailoring and garment work. Batches are kept small and timed around the day — morning, afternoon, and evening slots — so learners can fit classes around farm work, school, or housework.

Facilities

What's in the building

Modest, well-used, and kept running: two labs, backup power, and enough furniture that a full batch can sit and work at once.

Computer lab

Laptops, a shared printer and scanner, a whiteboard, and reference material including CCC and Rapidex course books.

Stitching room

Sewing machines with stands, an iron press, cutting tables, and the full kit of tailoring tools — scales, chalk, needles, and thread.

Power & connectivity

A solar inverter and stabilizer keep the labs running through outages, with a Jio Wi-Fi connection for the computer classes.

Seating & storage

Plastic chairs, laptop and stitching tables, and a large iron cupboard for materials and records.

Safety

A first-aid box and a multipurpose fire extinguisher are kept on site.

Records & outreach

Admission and visitor registers, attendance logs, and roughly 4,000 pamphlets used to reach new learners in nearby villages.
